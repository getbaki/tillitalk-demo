TilliTalk demo privacy

The demo stores daily learning time in your browser. No account or child name is requested. The website host may process technical request information such as IP addresses. Speech uses your browser or device speech service, which may process spoken text depending on its settings. The demo has no advertising or analytics code. Clearing browser site data removes the local timer record.
